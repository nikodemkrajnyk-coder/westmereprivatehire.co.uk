Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page lists everything westmereprivatehire.co.uk stores on your device, and why. It is a short list, which is the point: there are no advertising cookies here, no analytics, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

That is also why we do not show you a cookie banner. UK law requires your consent before a website stores anything on your device that is not strictly necessary for the service you asked for. Everything below is either strictly necessary or something you switched on yourself by ticking a box, so there is nothing for you to agree to. If that ever changes, we will ask properly.

1. The one cookie we set

NamePurposeDuration
wph_tokenKeeps you signed in to your account. Set only when you sign in, and deleted when you sign out. It is marked HttpOnly (scripts cannot read it), Secure (sent only over an encrypted connection) and SameSite (not sent to other websites). It contains a signed session token and nothing else — no name, no email, no booking details.30 days, or until you sign out

There are no other cookies. If you have never signed in, this site sets none at all.

2. Storage in your browser

Cookies are not the only way a website can keep something on your device. Our booking pages and apps use your browser's own local and session storage for the following, all of which stays on your device and is never sent anywhere:

WhatPurposeDuration
Unfinished bookingHolds what you have typed into a booking form — pickup, destination, time — so a page reload or a wrong tap does not lose it.Until the browser tab is closed
Remembered contact detailsYour name, phone and email, so the next booking form arrives filled in. Only if you tick "Remember my details on this device", which is off unless you turn it on. Untick it and it is deleted. No password or payment detail is ever kept this way.Until you untick the box or clear your browser
Your recent bookingsIn the customer app, a copy of your own journey list so the app opens with something on screen before it has finished talking to the server.Until you sign out
Display and dismissal preferencesSmall choices you have made — which view you last had open, an install prompt you dismissed — so you are not asked twice.Until you clear your browser
Sign-in attempt counterCounts failed sign-in attempts on this device so the form can slow down after several wrong passwords. A security measure.Minutes

3. Things loaded from other companies

No cookie is involved in any of these, but your browser does contact another company's servers, and that company will see your IP address:

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces this site is set in are loaded from Google's font servers.
  • Mapbox — map tiles in our booking apps are loaded directly from Mapbox.
  • Stripe — the card payment page is Stripe's own, and loads from Stripe.

Our privacy policy lists in full who we share data with and why.

4. Managing it yourself

Every browser lets you block or delete cookies and clear site storage from its settings — see aboutcookies.org for how, browser by browser. Clearing ours will sign you out and lose any remembered details and unfinished booking; nothing else will break.

5. Changes to this policy

If we add anything that stores data on your device, we will update this page — and if it is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent before setting it.
