Our story

Every journey should feel like a welcome.

Westmere is a premium private hire service based in Sussex, built on one straightforward belief: every journey should feel like a welcome. Professional drivers, immaculate luxury vehicles, and premium standards throughout — from the moment you book to the moment you arrive, you are in good hands.

Your driver will greet you by name, arrive on time, and get you there comfortably — with courtesy and no fuss. Confirmation is sent before every journey so you always know who is coming and when.

The Westmere standard

Personal, safe and always there.

We are not a pool of anonymous drivers. Every Westmere driver is personally vetted, fully licensed, and DBS checked — so you can travel with complete confidence, whether it is a solo airport run or a family transfer.

Good manners and clear communication are not extras — they are the foundation. Quiet, attentive, and always punctual: that is what Westmere means in practice.
